Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Nazarana values your privacy. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you use our website, purchase products or contact us.

Information We May Collect

We may collect your name, telephone number, email, billing and delivery address, order information, gift messages, correspondence and information you voluntarily provide. Our website and service providers may also process limited device, browser, IP address and website activity information where appropriate for functionality, security, analytics or other lawful purposes.

How We Use Information

We may use information to process and deliver orders; process transactions; communicate order information; provide customer support; prevent fraud or misuse; maintain business, tax and transaction records; comply with legal obligations; operate and improve our services; and send promotional communications where permitted and, where required, with appropriate consent.

Payments

Payments may be processed by independent payment providers. Where payment information is entered directly into their secure interface, Nazarana does not need to receive or store complete card, UPI or banking credentials. Their own policies may apply.

Sharing

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary with payment processors, logistics partners, technology providers and professional advisers, or where required by law or reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal rights. Nazarana does not sell customers' personal information as a business activity.

Retention & Security

Information may be retained as reasonably necessary for its purpose and legitimate business, accounting, tax, legal, dispute-resolution and regulatory requirements. We take reasonable administrative and technical security measures, but no internet or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your Choices & Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may contact Nazarana regarding your personal information, correction requests or other applicable rights. Where processing is based on consent, consent may be withdrawn subject to applicable law. Transactional communications necessary for existing orders may continue.

Third-Party Services & Updates

Independent third-party services are responsible for their own privacy practices. Nazarana may update this policy periodically; the latest published version will display its updated date.

Contact
